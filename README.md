# Pocket

Your personal money tracker. Works on your phone like an app, and all your money data stays on your phone.

## Put it online (one time)
1. Upload all the files in this folder to your GitHub repository **Pocket**: **Add file → Upload files**, drag them in, then click **Commit changes**.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Build and deployment", set **Source** to "Deploy from a branch" and **Branch** to `main` with folder `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
3. After 1–2 minutes your link is ready: **https://ary7n.github.io/Pocket/**

## Put it on your phone
- **iPhone:** open the link in **Safari** → tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in **Chrome** → tap **⋮** → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

Always open Pocket from the home-screen icon. The icon keeps its own data, separate from the browser.

## Updating later
Upload the new files the same way (they replace the old ones). Your data on the phone stays.

## Back up
In Pocket, go to **Settings → Save backup** once a month and keep the file somewhere safe (Files, Google Drive or email).
